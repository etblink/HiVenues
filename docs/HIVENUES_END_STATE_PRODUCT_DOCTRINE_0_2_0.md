## 0. Revision model

This document **inherits the 0.1.0 doctrine in full** except where this 0.2.0 revision adds, strengthens, or supersedes a rule below.

The purpose of this revision is to prevent a specific form of product drift exposed by owner review of the post-Astra-remediation screenshots: a technically coherent, visually improved, single-page-oriented product can still fall materially short of the actual HiVenues end state.

The 0.1.0 doctrine already established that HiVenues is a premium host-first frontend factory for Hive, that a HiVenue is a digital territory rather than merely a brochure, that composition families must be structurally distinct, that Studio must be a professional creative product, and that automated success cannot substitute for visual/product judgement.

0.2.0 makes those requirements harder to misread or satisfy prematurely.

---

# Part I — Reaffirmed North Star

## 1. The shortest product doctrine remains unchanged

> **The host’s world becomes the interface to Hive.**

HiVenues is a **premium, host-first frontend factory for Hive**.

It exists so that a nondeveloper can create and operate a distinctive digital territory whose public experience feels owned by the host while Hive supplies portable identity, content, social, community and economic primitives underneath where useful.

The product is not complete merely because it can generate a polished landing page.

---

## 2. The 2024 founding prototype is a normative design reference, not a literal specification

The original public HiVenues concept demonstrated an important product principle that remains controlling:

- a conventional host/business experience came first;
- Hive-backed community and content appeared inside that host experience rather than as a separate blockchain product;
- feeds, post detail, profile/wall/inbox concepts, voting/resource metaphors and contextual value/payment actions were presented as parts of the host’s world;
- mechanics could be translated into a host-specific visual language without changing their actual consequence.

The old prototype does **not** freeze its exact UI, terminology, technology or information architecture.

It **does** freeze the ambition level: HiVenues must be capable of producing a real host-specific application/frontend, not only an attractive brochure.

---

# Part II — Digital territory means a real frontend application

## 3. Multi-page and route-level architecture is first-class

A mature HiVenue may be one page when that is genuinely the strongest truthful experience for a sparse host.

The platform itself must not be one-page-only.

The semantic system and rendering architecture must support coherent route/page families such as, where appropriate:

```text
Home
Activities / Activity detail
Offers / Menu / Catalog
Community / Feed
Post / Discussion detail
Gallery / Media
People / Profiles
Profile / Wall / Inbox when admitted
About / Visit / Contact
Account / Rewards / Resource / Wallet-like host-native surfaces when admitted
```

A Direction may collapse, expand, rename, reorder or visually reinterpret these surfaces.

A feature-rich host must not be forced into one infinitely long page merely because the implementation began that way.

Public route/page structure is a projection of canonical semantic objects. It must not become a second page-owned data model.

---

## 4. Social, community and economic capability belong inside the frontend factory

The end-state capability envelope includes, when appropriate to a host:

- host updates and short-form feed content;
- long-form posts and post detail;
- comments/replies/discussion;
- profile/member/patron identity;
- follow and community participation;
- host-native voting/recommendation/support mechanics;
- resource/reward/value state translated into host language;
- private or directed communication surfaces where a product requirement justifies them;
- payment/tip/purchase/support flows behind explicit authority and consequence boundaries;
- durable Activities, Offers, media, galleries, feeds and related open projections.

No host needs all of these.

HiVenues must nevertheless be architecturally and experientially capable of them without turning every host into the same portal or exposing a generic Hive dashboard.

---

# Part III — Differentiation must become falsifiable

## 5. Direction is broader than a composition family label

Poster, Editorial and Hospitality remain useful early proof families.

They are not a sufficient final catalog and must not become the permanent boundary of the product.

A mature Direction system may contain multiple compositions/recipes within one broad art direction.

Meaningful variation may include:

- navigation model;
- route/page topology;
- hero grammar;
- section grouping;
- information hierarchy;
- typography system;
- density and whitespace;
- surface/border/shape language;
- media choreography;
- card/list/timeline/gallery treatment;
- interaction placement;
- icon/illustration language;
- footer behavior;
- desktop/mobile reflow;
- how social/community/value surfaces are embedded in the host world.

Shared semantics do **not** require shared page skeletons or shared DOM hierarchy.

Reuse that makes unrelated hosts visually converge is a product defect, not an architectural virtue.

---

## 6. Same-host multi-direction proof is a mandatory end-state test

Before HiVenues can be considered ready for a serious independent end-state beta, the same **feature-saturated synthetic host** must be rendered through materially different Directions and compositions.

The canonical host data must remain constant while the presentation changes.

The comparison should answer:

> **Did HiVenues create genuinely different professional digital territories from the same semantic truth, or did it create skins around one layout?**

This is stronger than comparing unrelated fixtures because it isolates the composition system itself.

A Direction/composition system that cannot pass this test is not mature enough for the final product bar.

---

## 7. The synthetic archetype suite precedes the first real customer

Fourth Street Bar is the first real customer.

It is **not** the first specimen that should bear the burden of proving the full product.

Before the Fourth Street clean-room customer trial, HiVenues must first pass internal qualification and then an independent synthetic-first campaign using:

1. one feature-saturated fictional reference host exercising every mutually compatible end-state capability;
2. that same host across multiple Directions and compositions;
3. several additional fictional hosts chosen to stress materially different information architectures, such as hospitality/nightlife, creator/artist, publication, community/organization, conference/gallery or other relevant archetypes.

Only after that synthetic campaign is independently accepted should the first real customer become the next gate.

---

# Part IV — Studio completion means full frontend authorship

## 8. Studio must author the territory, not merely the home page

The Studio must progressively expose control over the complete host experience, including as applicable:

- host identity and presence;
- navigation and route/page structure;
- story/about/editorial content;
- Activities and Offers;
- galleries and role-aware media;
- community/feed/post/discussion surfaces;
- profile/member/patron surfaces;
- participation and Voice;
- Hive/provider bindings;
- host-native resource/reward/value representations;
- integrations and provider health/degraded states;
- Releases, History, Restore, conflicts and urgent operations.

The control model remains semantic and constrained.

“Complete” does not mean arbitrary source, arbitrary CSS or arbitrary DOM editing.

---

## 9. Studio mental model

The mature Studio should make a progression equivalent to the following legible without imposing rigid navigation labels:

> **Create → Shape → Participate → Review → Release → Operate**

The operator should spend most of their time looking at the rendered host world.

Common edits should be direct/contextual.

Deep controls should be progressively disclosed.

Operational and consequence-heavy workflows should be calm, explicit and separate from ordinary creative manipulation.

There remains one canonical host and one durable working state throughout.

---

# Part V — Visual language and iconography

## 10. SVG iconography is a first-class design-system capability

HiVenues should use coherent vector iconography where it improves recognition, density, navigation or polish.

Appropriate Studio uses may include:

- navigation and command actions;
- media/gallery controls;
- review-width/device-representation controls;
- Undo/Redo, History and Release state;
- calendar/location/contact semantics;
- social/community mechanics;
- wallet/value/support mechanics;
- success/warning/pending/degraded states.

Rules:

- icons supplement meaning; they must not create mystery controls;
- icon-only controls require accessible names and strong keyboard/focus/touch behavior;
- avoid decorative icon soup;
- prefer a coherent reusable SVG component/symbol contract over one-off glyphs;
- Studio utility iconography and public host-native iconography are different concerns;
- public host-native mechanics may use more expressive custom SVG/illustration/metaphor when truthful and appropriate to the Direction/Voice.

Raster images should not be used for ordinary UI chrome when a semantic vector control is appropriate.

# Part VI — Architecture is a product-quality enabler

## 11. The rendering architecture must preserve semantic truth without homogenizing outputs

The canonical implementation direction is defined in the companion:

`docs/HIVENUES_RENDERING_AUTHORING_ARCHITECTURE_DOCTRINE_0_1_0.md`

The key product-level rules are:

- durable application truth remains server-owned;
- semantic HTML is a first-class output;
- reusable semantic components and fragments are encouraged;
- composition-specific structures are equally encouraged when needed for differentiation;
- reuse must not force one universal DOM/page skeleton;
- responsive behavior should be authored at the correct level: component-space behavior separately from actual viewport/device-shell behavior;
- client-side interaction must not become a second durable host-state model.

This architecture is subordinate to the product doctrine. If a framework convenience pushes HiVenues toward generic templates or a parallel client model, the product doctrine wins.

# Part VII — Quality and qualification

## 12. “Professional 2026 website” is an acceptance requirement

A generated HiVenue should be credible beside strong independent commercial websites/applications in the host’s category without requiring the viewer to lower expectations because the site came from a generator.

The standard includes, where appropriate:

- excellent typography;
- deliberate hierarchy;
- mature navigation;
- real-media choreography;
- coherent route/page structure;
- polished sparse and dense states;
- strong desktop/mobile composition;
- accessible interaction;
- purposeful motion/micro-interaction;
- visually integrated social/community/value surfaces;
- no obvious universal HiVenues section skeleton;
- no generic SaaS or blockchain residue.

The Studio must independently pass a comparable quality bar as a professional creative product.

Green tests prove protected contracts. They do not prove this visual/product bar.

---

## 13. Artifact review is a hard gate before independent beta

An independent Astra cycle must not be used to rediscover obvious incompleteness already visible in exact-head screenshots and internal product review.

Before opening an end-state synthetic beta, Project Lead must review exact-head evidence covering at least:

- desktop Studio normal/contextual/review/release states;
- native narrow/touch Studio states;
- feature-saturated synthetic host;
- same-host multi-Direction/composition comparison;
- multiple additional synthetic archetypes;
- representative multi-page/route surfaces;
- community/social/profile/value surfaces included in current end-state scope;
- real media and logo treatment;
- sparse and dense states;
- accessibility, overflow and media integrity;
- Release/draft/history/urgent/conflict behavior;
- no unauthorized external effects.

The Project Lead must be willing to state that the build resembles the intended product closely enough that independent testing will expose non-obvious operator failures rather than unfinished design work.

# Part VIII — Drift checks added in 0.2.0

## 14. Additional mandatory questions for major work

The 0.1.0 doctrine check remains in force and gains these additional questions:

13. Are we building a real frontend territory or merely a longer landing page?
14. Can the same rich host produce materially different professional compositions without losing semantic truth?
15. Are social/community/value capabilities integrated into the host world rather than bolted on as a separate Hive dashboard?
16. Are we reusing semantic components while still allowing structurally distinct composition markup?
17. Does responsive behavior adapt at both component and viewport levels rather than relying on one global breakpoint system?
18. Are Studio controls becoming more legible through coherent iconography where appropriate without becoming icon-only mystery UI?
19. Would this exact-head build pass an internal screenshot review before we spend an independent operator cycle on it?
20. Have synthetic fixtures, rather than the first real customer, already exercised the full intended capability surface?

A major phase that cannot answer these questions convincingly is not end-state convergence.
